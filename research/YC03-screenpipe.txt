DECISION AND REVIEW BOUNDARY

Screenpipe is a strong reference for the context, evidence and workflow-learning layer of SupaBiz. Its most useful contribution is the connection between observed work, traceable suggestions, human correction and reusable procedures. Adopt those interaction patterns first using our existing message and email intake. Consider optional desktop awareness later, scoped to a clearly valuable workflow.

This is a deep selected-source review of one pinned revision, not an audit of all 5,904 tracked files, a shipped-binary reverse engineering exercise or proof of production performance. The REA skill routes complete public source to ordinary repository analysis. The application was not installed or launched; no real screen, microphone, account, message history or credential was accessed. Twelve checks executed copied source helper functions with synthetic data and fake responses. Rust capture, storage and security paths were read but not executed. The two specialist appendices preserve further UX and privacy evidence.

Current source uses a commercial source-available license. Our recommendation distinguishes independently implementing ideas from embedding upstream code or operating its product under suitable terms. No licensed-integration decision has been made. The previously saved Executor and Agent37 findings remain intact; all recommendations are design candidates, not shipped SupaBiz functionality.

WHERE IT FITS WITH EXECUTOR AND AGENT37

Executor informs integration discovery, account connections and authorized tool execution. Agent37 informs persistent execution environments, work continuity, outputs and browser handoff. Screenpipe informs how context is observed, indexed, retrieved, cited and corrected. SupaBiz must own business records, permissions, identities, deadlines, workflow versions, approval receipts and verified outcomes across all three.

The proposed flow is: primary WhatsApp/email connectors plus optional scoped desktop observations → immutable evidence and source-health records → permission-aware retrieval → domain extraction and clarification → versioned work card → authorized action → result verification. The agent runtime consumes this context and executes bounded jobs; it is not the only home of business truth.

Keep three identities distinct: the historical observation, the current business record, and a proposed operation. A screenshot can show a draft invoice, but that does not mean the invoice was sent or booked. A previous button location cannot authorize a click today. Reobserve the live application, bind the target and account, recheck permissions, and verify the external result after execution.

WHAT AN OWNER OR OPERATOR WOULD EXPERIENCE

Ramya opens a Needs attention view. A card says: Possible hearing-date change; matter identity unresolved; email received at 10:42; source and attachment available. She opens Show why, sees the relevant passage and corrects the matter or date by typing or voice. Her edit wins over a late model response. SupaBiz records the correction against that exact version, shows the proposed assignee/calendar change, and asks for approval only where policy requires it. If another update arrives first, the old action becomes stale and requires reconciliation.

For Varun, a kitchen photo and message become a proposed maintenance item under Consu, with an owner and a next check. Later changes the reminder, not the underlying promised completion time. Not right can correct the outlet, classify the issue differently or dismiss the suggestion. Reopen preserves the previous decision. Completion requires the agreed evidence: for example, a follow-up photo and manager confirmation. An inventory entry needs the product, unit, quantity, outlet and source; repeated similar messages are not automatically duplicate stock movements.

The second valuable experience is Teach SupaBiz this workflow. Ramya demonstrates a sample intake process using synthetic matter data. The system drafts steps and shows which observation supports each step. It asks only missing questions: how to distinguish a new matter, who owns this class of case, which timezone controls a deadline, what requires principal review, and what to do when an attachment is missing. The reviewed draft is versioned and tested before limited automation. A later change proposes a revision rather than silently rewriting the approved procedure.

The third experience is honest awareness. Users see which source is enabled, what was last successfully indexed, where coverage has gaps and what is private to their device. A paused microphone is not a broken connector. A healthy local process does not prove an email inbox is current. A record removed from search is not necessarily erased from a backup.

IMPLEMENTATION SEQUENCE AND ACCEPTANCE GATES

Stage 1 — Evidence and work cards. Use WhatsApp/email fixtures and existing connector contracts. Build source IDs, immutable references, record versions and the action ledger. Add Show why, correct, snooze, dismiss and reopen. Test stale versions, duplicate arrival, deletion, missing attachments and wrong-business access. Gate: every proposed action has traceable evidence and stale actions cannot silently apply.

Stage 2 — Workflow teaching. Add a structured procedure schema, evidence-linked steps, missing-information questions and voice-assisted fields. Preserve manual edits, support uncertainty and publish through version checks. Test the Ramya intake and Varun maintenance flows with ambiguous dates, near-identical matters, accented speech and changed procedures. Gate: each procedure can be replayed against synthetic cases and rejected safely when its preconditions are missing.

Stage 3 — Optional desktop pilot. Begin with an explicit app/domain allowlist, visible capture indicator and pause. Prefer accessibility to OCR; add bounded queues, durable acknowledgement and source-instance identity. Keep raw observations local unless released by policy. Use artificial desktops and media for exclusion, private-window, monitor-change, permission-revocation and sleep/resume tests on each supported OS. Gate: capture exclusions and cross-business access tests pass before any real client rollout.

Stage 4 — Safe execution and scale. Connect approved procedures to the runtime and a single integration policy authority. Add idempotency, budgets, retry/reconciliation and remote result verification. Load-test realistic bursts and long retention, then size storage and compute from measurements. Gate: retries cannot duplicate stock movements or calendar changes, uncertain results remain visible, and restoration preserves provenance/grants.

These are dependency stages, not calendar promises. Sprint length depends on the existing SupaBiz implementation, which this review has not re-audited. Prioritize a narrow intake-to-assignment or photo-to-maintenance outcome before broad desktop recording. Do not move to app-to-app agent collaboration until business identity and action authority are stable.

PERFORMANCE, COMPLEXITY AND COST WITHOUT INVENTED BENCHMARKS

Let F be capture candidates, P pixels per image, N stored observations, H query matches, K returned results, L snippet length, D source branches and o pagination offset. A fixed-rate baseline processes approximately rT candidates over duration T. Event-triggering plus idle fallback changes the candidate count; AX-first and successful durable hash gating reduce expensive OCR calls further. Image hashing still costs O(P) on inspected pixels; OCR cost depends on resolution and engine. Per-app last-state gates use O(A) hashes plus cached text across A apps; they do not remember every prior image.

The database writer uses a bounded 4096-entry channel and batches up to 500, amortizing transaction overhead. Queue memory also depends on each payload size. A full queue needs an explicit backpressure/recovery policy; bounded memory is not proof of zero loss. Two filesystems/database writes are not automatically one atomic cross-resource transaction. Monitor recovery around screenshot creation, database commit and cursor advancement.

Full-text search depends on index selectivity, postings and query planning; it is not universally O(log N). Mixed search can fetch D(K+o) candidates, then sort them in roughly O(M log M) for M collected candidates. Stable cursor pagination and earlier authorized filters are preferable for deep histories. Per-hit truncation limits ordinary content to approximately O(KL), but explicit unlimited settings, metadata and multiple calls still require an outer aggregate budget.

Lazy screenshot loading changes network/image work from all workflow steps to visible steps and candidate images. It improves demand but does not prove a global concurrency bound. Voice clarification introduces transcription/model latency and correction work; user edits need revision protection. Workflow timing sorting is O(r log r) over at most 30 intervals, but those intervals estimate elapsed time, not labor or business value.

The attached cost model is deliberately synthetic: eight hours, one baseline frame per second versus an assumed 180 changed frames per hour, 150,000 bytes per image, and one 64 kb/s audio track. It yields 28,800 versus 1,440 candidate images and 4.32 versus 0.216 decimal GB of image payload per day. The 95% candidate reduction follows only from these assumptions; it is not measured screenpipe performance. Audio adds 0.2304 GB/day in this scenario. Thirty days totals 136.512 versus 13.392 GB before indexes, metadata, replicas, backups and compression differences. Multiply for tracks, monitors and devices as applicable; event fallback can change the counts.

Price decisions need license/hosting terms, actual awake device CPU/energy, support, cloud storage, egress, ASR and model rates. Local-first saves cloud traffic only to the extent raw context stays local; it can increase device and support costs. There is no verified all-in price or productivity improvement percentage from this review. Measure p50/p95 capture-to-search and source-to-task latency, CPU/RAM/disk, battery drain, OCR/ASR quality, correction time, missed tasks, duplicate actions and total cost per verified business outcome. Compare baseline and proposed approach on the same labeled workload and report sample sizes, failures and uncertainty.

RISKS, UNKNOWNS AND NEXT COMPARISON

Privacy and enterprise isolation need independent implementation and tests. Source review found a conditional mismatch between accepted credential transports and pipe permission extraction, a short autogenerated API key, important differences among local redaction/remote confidential processing, and retention modes that preserve searchable text. The privacy appendix records exact conditions and references; none is presented as a live exploit or a comprehensive security audit.

Unverified: full platform capture coverage, deployment-specific permission and account isolation, actual latency/battery/storage under Indian SME workloads, recognition quality, end-to-end revocation, backup deletion, hosted controls and commercial licensing fit. Official or source comments about speed are not our benchmarks. A source check passing does not establish production reliability.

The practical recommendation is to adopt evidence navigation, reversible versioned decisions, procedure learning and honest source status now as product design. Experiment with selective desktop context and modality routing after connectors and business records work. Defer blanket continuous recording and broad agent-to-agent coordination. The next queued one-by-one review is Asakana, which can test whether our WhatsApp-to-record-to-review workflow is concrete enough; it has not been started in this task.
