Product contract

One clear promise. A controlled path to action.

Help a business see what is pending, who owns it, and the evidence behind it; then safely coordinate and verify the next action.

User value

Nothing silently falls through

See pending work, its owner, source evidence and missing information. Keep WhatsApp/email where people already work.

System boundary

AI proposes. The system governs.

Models interpret and suggest. Accepted records, current permissions, durable work and verification determine what happens.

Delivery discipline

One workflow earns the next

Start with a real buyer and a bounded shadow trial. Expand after value, reliability and total review cost are demonstrated.

This is the foundational blueprint, not a completion report. The research and website exist; the actual product code, connector coverage, customer demand and operating metrics still need audit or field evidence.

Reprocessing the debate

Keep the funnel. Sharpen the decisions.

Preserve

Same 12 stations; Foundation 1.0 intact

The factory structure and platform vision remain. The new edition refines mechanisms and delivery gates; it does not turn every responsibility into pilot scope.

Repair

Two different coverage checks

Independent inventory versus received events measures observed source coverage. Received revisions versus processing dispositions measures conservation. Unknown denominators and aged processing backlog stay visible.

Add

C17: authority per field

Name authority, external identity/version, observation health, write mode and cutover. New external edits stale dependent proposals and checks.

Clarify

Cancellation before and after transmission

A durable transmit marker is evidence of possible transmission, not proof of it. Serialize local fencing; reconcile outside effects and use evidence-backed UI labels.

Strengthen

Instruction semantics and review capacity

Explicit override authority, scope, time boundaries and change classes; consequential holds are not automatically safe. Backlogs escalate rather than silently auto-accept.

Bound

Runner and worker capabilities

An owned versioned runner charter limits custom infrastructure. Parser, intake and effect workers have distinct enforced capability profiles.

Separate

Vendor cost, customer burden and combined cost

Customer time is not automatically a vendor cash expense. Model three views, count subscription as a transfer in combined economics, and label price assumptions.

Expose

Debate, provenance and untested implementation

A twelve-decision log, declarative failure registry, code-audit template and source-path index show what changed, why and what has not been demonstrated.

The prior 76 packages are an inherited inventory of unequal responsibilities, not 76 services or equal units of effort. New instruction and pilot contracts extend the semantics; they do not create a thirteenth station.

Recommendations we qualified or rejected

  • One event → one candidate: replaced with revision accounting and many-to-many evidence.
  • After claim means too late: replaced with fenced before-send marker, conservative unknown outcomes and verified cancellation.
  • Restrictive instructions are safe: rejected; delay, urgency and consequence remain part of risk.
  • Canaries alone establish rare-event recall: rejected; known-path tests and natural observations are reported separately.
  • Natural data alone is mandatory/sufficient for reliance: replaced with an authorized, risk-specific assurance case.
  • Rank or specificity silently overrides a hold: rejected; explicit override capability/target required.
  • Opening evidence proves review quality: rejected; use outcome-oriented review exercises and spot checks.
  • Every linked session has identical scope, or Gmail label selection is provider-enforced: unverified assumptions; inspect each adapter.
  • No electronic API means no usable evidence: qualified; dated authorized scans/counts/attestations can support limited checks.
  • A paid commitment must precede discovery: rejected; keep bounded discovery separate from demand and reliance gates.

Browser, vision, voice & video

Different capabilities. The same evidence and action rules.

Voice

Capture intent without a form

Staff voice → transcript with language and timestamps → cited candidate. An authenticated owner command still needs scoped authority and confirmation where required. Spoken output is a presentation choice; it must disclose uncertainty.

Vision

Read what is visible

Photo/document → original + derivative regions → proposed observations. OCR and image models do not establish hidden conditions. Units, dates and record identity get independent checks.

Video

Bound the observation

Use transcript, metadata and selected frames for a specific task; preserve sampled intervals and missed coverage. Long recordings consume time and storage. Continuous cameras remain deferred until a proven workflow warrants them.

Browser

Retrieve or execute in a scoped session

Read authorized sites or complete an approved action when an API is inadequate. Pages are untrusted content. Separate read and write capabilities, isolate credentials, constrain destinations and record outcome uncertainty. A browser screenshot is not proof that a transaction settled.

Desktop awareness is optional source collection; cloud browser execution is a tool environment. Neither should imply broad screen recording or a permanent VM for every customer.

Economics & scale

Optimize completed work, including human time.

Three economic views prevent customer effort from being mistaken for vendor cash cost. Change assumptions below. Prices, volumes and results are illustrative; no benefits, willingness to pay or operating margin have been measured.

Three views / illustrative monthly model

The same review hour belongs to its actual payer. Subscription is excluded from combined resource cost; no customer benefit is assumed.

  • All source volumes and review hours are totals across the businesses; price and business count calculate subscription revenue. Price is a placeholder for sensitivity analysis, not a G0 pricing anchor.
  • Provider/operator review belongs in vendor cash only when GoFathom pays it. Customer review is opportunity cost at an explicit valuation, not an automatic cash expense. Do not count the same reviewer twice.
  • Vendor contribution = subscription revenue minus modeled vendor cash; excludes taxes, sales, founder salaries, onboarding and omitted costs. It is not net profit.
  • Customer burden = subscription plus customer review opportunity cost; benefit has not been measured. Combined resource cost excludes subscription because it is a transfer between the parties.
  • Cost per verified completion uses genuinely completed outcomes; zero completions is undefined. Simulated/default values never establish real margins or improvement.
