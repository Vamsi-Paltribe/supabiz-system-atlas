SUPABIZ CROSS-PROJECT SYNTHESIS — YC07–YC14
17 October 2026 | Derived from the saved reviews, not a new vendor audit

The eight projects are Sidekick, Marble, Agent Relay, Glen, Decawork, GrepAI, herdr and Terse. Their central objects differ: frontline report, operational transaction, inter-agent message, organizational knowledge, proposed action, reusable procedure, running session and persistent shared object respectively. These are complementary responsibilities; they are not eight interchangeable agent frameworks or eight mandatory dependencies.

1. Conversation is an entry point; structured, accountable work is the intended destination. A report should become a record, an owner and a verified outcome. Sidekick informs capture and follow-up, Marble supplies domain transactions, and GrepAI organizes repeatable work.

2. Separate knowledge, authoritative business records, execution state and external outcomes. Glen, herdr and Terse address different forms of continuity. Calling all of them memory hides permission, durability and truth boundaries. A phone reconnect must recover the current run and business state rather than repeat an instruction.

3. Separate receipt, delivery, observation, approval and verified completion. A Relay delivery acknowledgement cannot prove a supplier order exists. A recalled memory cannot establish current truth. A Decawork-style approval cannot establish successful execution. An idle terminal cannot prove completion. Saved actor state cannot establish an external provider's outcome.

4. Make exceptions and decisions the manager interface. Combine simple staff capture, role-specific work queues, clear reason/evidence cards and a change history. Reading a card must not approve it or resolve its underlying work. Owners need cross-business scope labels and a trustworthy next action.

5. Improvement is several mechanisms: reviewed knowledge, better instructions, model selection and evaluated executable procedures. It is not automatically model retraining. Promote a scoped candidate only after tests and authorization; preserve version and rollback. Business facts must still be retrieved and permissions rechecked on every run.

6. Borrow responsibilities before adopting platforms. Begin with identity, evidence, authoritative records, durable jobs and controlled effects. Place useful patterns behind small contracts. Add specialist runtime infrastructure only when equal-correctness experiments show a worthwhile operational or performance advantage.

EVIDENCE CAUTION
Some repetition reflects our consistent SupaBiz questions and recommendations, not independently observed convergence in vendor implementations. Sidekick, Marble and Decawork were public-product reviews; other reviews exposed selected clients or source paths with explicit limitations. No complete application audit was claimed. No shared performance percentage or all-vendor guarantee follows from these reviews.

EXAMPLE
Varun's outcome is a reconciled delivery with the correct accepted quantity and invoice exception. Ramya's outcome is a current authorized assignment with correct documents and deadline. These outcomes determine record schemas, permission checks and acceptance tests; they should not be defined by which vendor tool happens to be available.
