Block expansion

Critical failures

Cross-business disclosure, unsupported critical amount/status/action, duplicate effect, stale approval or silently lost eligible work blocks expansion. “Unknown” everywhere cannot pass when clear evidence was available.

Keep uncertainty explicit

No invented success rate

Freeze thresholds and denominators before evaluation. Separate natural observations, protected fault tests and known-path probes. Runtime correctness and customer savings are not proved by reviewer agreement or test counts.

Retain current TypeScript/PostgreSQL/worker foundation until source audit demonstrates a concrete gap; do not migrate to an agent framework to treat semantic boundary defects.

Cost and scaling: what still needs measurement+

Chat reports large-history context (~37k input tokens per answer in one experiment) and excessive reprocessing; source complexity cannot be established here.

For history bytes H and changed bytes Δ plus required context C, extraction should process Δ+C rather than H where dependencies allow. Corrections may require a larger affected dependency closure. Indexed lookup cost depends on query/index/selectivity; exact retrieval remains bounded by returned evidence bytes. Storage grows with source/derivative/revision retention; do not claim constant memory or guaranteed speedup.

Separate vendor cash cost, customer review effort and combined resource cost. Compare total ingestion plus answering and review effort at equivalent correctness; no percentage gain is established.
