2. The valid Rs 1,152 invoice rejected for comma formatting or repeated evidence references.
3. Invoice lines continuing to contribute after rejection of their parent.

6. Wrong-outlet forwards, same-name people, ambiguous units and table-to-item mappings.
7. Unknown event time, late corrections and distinct document/effective/approval dates.
