knowledge_lifecycle:
  id:
    KB01
  name:
    Nominate
  rule:
    Create a brief only for an active workflow, repeated question or useful entity; retain an explicit reason. Do not summarize every message into every entity.
  fields:
    Entity ID, business/matter scope, audience class, intended use, source references and creation reason.
  test:
    An unrelated new supplier message does not rewrite every outlet brief.

  id:
    KB02
  name:
    Compile
  rule:
    Synthesize across eligible episodes. Label accepted fact, tentative interpretation, scoped preference, approved procedure and narrative separately. Critical quantities/statuses come from the shared version-bound result.
  fields:
    Brief ID/version, claim or section dependencies, source spans/timestamps, record revisions, model/extractor version, coverage and unresolved questions.
  test:
    Two repeated summaries of one complaint still represent one underlying observation.

  id:
    KB03
  name:
    Invalidate
  rule:
    A matching correction, backfill, processed attachment, extractor/policy change or grant change marks affected claims for revalidation. Use reverse dependencies plus scoped selectors for absence claims. A source timestamp alone is insufficient.
  fields:

  cost:
    Total = intake + extraction + indexing + initial compilation + refresh/invalidation + retrieval + answering + retries + human review. Report storage/media/index growth, queue age, p50/p95 latency, correction-to-safe-read delay and model/provider charges separately.
  decision:
    Keep the brief only if it improves useful answers or repeated-work effort at an acceptable measured maintenance cost without weakening correctness or access controls. Thresholds depend on pilot workload and must be agreed before evaluating protected cases. No claimed percentage improvement.
  complexity:
    With N stored objects, E dependency edges and B retained source bytes, base storage is O(B + N + E) before media/index/vector overhead. Invalidating D affected descendants through E_D relevant reverse edges is O(D + E_D); selector lookup for absence claims adds work. Broad rule changes can touch most objects. Retrieval depends on indexes, selectivity and permissions; no constant-time promise. Model compilation cost depends on input/output tokens and update fan-out.
  breakeven:
    For a period, maintained knowledge is cheaper only when Q × (baseline per-query cost − brief-assisted per-query cost) exceeds initial compilation plus all update/maintenance costs. This is an analytical condition, not a measured result; quality, privacy and human effort must also pass.

FAILURE TESTS:
  [1]
    case: FT01 · Captured-event conservation
    stimulus: Replay one source revision twice; leave another pending; quarantine one; exclude one with a rule; let one message create three candidates and a burst support one candidate.
    expected: Captured source revisions reconcile once across pending/excluded/quarantined/failed/assessed states. Candidate links are many-to-many; no excluded event disappears from audit.

  [2]
    case: FT02 · Independent denominator and canary limits
    stimulus: Remove an eligible event before journal capture and another at routing; compare a separately sourced inventory. Include shared-failure and unknown-inventory variants.
    expected: Report observed omission or unknown denominator. Labeled real-event recall and known canary detection are separate; processing reconciliation alone never proves source completeness.

  [3]
    case: FT03 · Authority revision conflict
    stimulus: Change an external field after a check is proposed and before admission/writeback; edit the former authority after cutover.
    expected: Stale bound check cannot authorize overwriting the new value. Re-read/review or reconcile; dual-authority edits raise a visible exception.

  [4]
    case: FT04 · Revoke and cancel at each boundary
    stimulus: Revoke before claim, after claim/before send, during send, after remote commit and after lost receipt; race two dispatchers.
    expected: No claim after committed scope stop. Earlier claims receive truthful prevented/requested/verified-cancelled/completed/unknown labels. At most one valid local claim per intent generation; provider effects reconciled.

  [5]
    case: FT05 · Unknown effect and compensation
    stimulus: Provider commits then times out; a retry arrives; later compensation is requested.
    expected: No blind duplicate action; reconcile first. Compensation requires its own current authorization and receipt. Unknown is never silently relabeled failed or cancelled.

  [6]
    case: FT06 · Instruction semantics and override
    stimulus: Apply a supplier hold; propose narrower subordinate release; use “until Friday” without boundary; supersede after dispatch claim.
    expected: Unauthorized release and ambiguous boundary are held. Explicit authorized overrides trace their target; prospective rules do not undo an already emitted effect. A model cannot invent a preauthorized instruction policy entry; affected-work preview names its coverage limits.

  [7]
    case: FT07 · Review backlog and exact confirmation
    stimulus: Exceed planned review capacity; submit two similar proposals; issue an ambiguous “OK”.
    expected: Backlog and age remain visible, escalation has an owner and nothing auto-accepts. Ambiguous confirmation binds to no proposal until clarified. Critical work is not hidden by budget caps.

  [8]
    case: FT08 · Runner charter and upgrade
    stimulus: Crash after lease claim; run a stale worker; restart a timer; deploy an unknown job definition; request a new unsupported orchestration primitive.
    expected: Fence stale worker, preserve bounded timer/retry progress, hold unsupported definitions and trigger a runner/engine review. Reconcile effect uncertainty outside retry guessing.

  [9]
    case: FT09 · Parser boundary and source minimization
    stimulus: Attempt prohibited file/network access and resource exhaustion; inject nonselected chat content into a controlled adapter fixture; inspect storage/log/cache/crash paths.
    expected: Permitted derivative still works; prohibited access is denied. Document actual prefilter exposure and measured persistence. Reject a deployment whose agreed privacy boundary cannot be met.

  [10]
    case: FT10 · Frozen experiment and model qualification
    stimulus: Change eligibility after results, swap model/parser, use unavailable observer and expose oracle answers to acting model.
    expected: Original results remain; amended experiment has new digest/window. Model swap reruns qualified cases; unobservable episode is ungradable; oracle leakage invalidates evaluation. A changed or expired reliance decision prevents expansion of the allowed automation scope.

  [11]
    case: FT11 · Human multi-business isolation
    stimulus: A CA/operator switches businesses, reuses a cached result and then loses one grant; try sharing under a common owner.
    expected: Active context and current scoped grants govern reads/exports; cache key and invalidation cannot substitute for authorization. Cross-business disclosure needs an explicit allowed purpose.

  [12]
    case: FT12 · Required modalities and citations
    stimulus: Feed permitted blurred photo, noisy Hinglish audio, text-only email and sampled video as required by the chosen workflow.
    expected: Return supported cited candidates or visible uncertainty; modality coverage and wrong-extraction/abstention reported per risk slice; no unsupported hidden-condition inference.

COMPLEXITY:
  [1]
    path: Entity lookup
    baseline: O(N) scan; naive all-pairs matching O(N²)
    approach: Indexed exact lookup O(log N + k), then bounded candidate checks
    space: O(N) index plus record storage
    limit: Fuzzy matching can still approach all-pairs behavior; cap candidates and measure ambiguity.

  [2]
    path: Source dedupe / journal
    baseline: Repeated work on every redelivery; no bounded completion guarantee
    approach: B-tree identity lookup/insert O(log E); O(E) total retained events
    space: O(E + media bytes)
    limit: Database contention, revisions and retention alter real throughput; stable provider identity is required.

  [3]
    path: Context assembly
    baseline: Send all retained history, O(T) tokens per request
    approach: Retrieve k relevant items, bound assembled tokens B; permission checks on all selected items
    space: O(N) search index plus bounded per-run context
    limit: No constant-time retrieval guarantee; loss of recall must be measured. LLM attention cost depends on model implementation.

  [4]
    path: Image / video processing
    baseline: Process all F frames or whole assets at full resolution
    approach: Process selected f frames or relevant regions, f ≤ F
    space: O(retained assets + derivatives)
    limit: Work often scales with processed bytes/frames but sampling may miss events; no accuracy equivalence assumed.

  [5]
    path: Work board / timers
    baseline: Repeated full scan of all tasks N
    approach: Index by tenant/status/due time: O(log N + k) for page or due batch
    space: O(N) task/timer state
    limit: Worst-case many due tasks still require O(N) work; fair scheduling and concurrency limits matter.

  [6]
    path: Version-guarded transition
    baseline: Long AI computation while holding locks
    approach: AI outside transaction; short conditional update and transactional outbox
    space: O(history + pending work)
    limit: Retries under contention and remote latency remain; no global exactly-once claim.

  title:
    Episode identity
  description:
    Stable ID plus versioned membership edges to evidence and typed records. Tasks reference specific records and task versions. Matching document keys retrieve candidates; they do not authorize merge. Split/merge keeps lineage and revalidates affected approvals.

  title:
    Retrieval and routing
  description:
    Use reply chains, scoped identities and document identifiers first. Expand through bounded lexical, entity, time and semantic search where useful, including closed situations. Log search-or-skip reasons; audit skipped events independently. SQL candidates do not replace language interpretation.

  title:
    Changing evidence
  description:
    Positive dependencies cover accepted records, calculations and consequential model-derived interpretations. Negative results retain selector scope and observation watermark. New matching evidence can invalidate a conclusion without correcting any earlier record.

  title:
    Temporal meaning
  description:
    Keep event time, source/document time, effective time and decision time distinct. Unknown dates remain unknown. A current instruction must not be silently dropped because no historical effective date was supplied; activation follows scoped instruction policy.

  title:
    Authority and concurrency
  description:
    Evidence, interpretation, accepted state and authority remain distinct. Current grants and relevant source/effect/policy versions are checked at admission or execution. Re-review is necessary when those change; otherwise avoid redundant approval.

  title:
    Owner experience
  description:
    Needs attention, Work in progress, Ask GoFathom and What GoFathom knows remain proposed surfaces. Cards show evidence, uncertainty, recommended action and exact effect. Investigate first, route to the best-placed authorized person, batch nonurgent questions and expose aging.

  title:
    Tool and harness boundary
  description:
    The harness supplies context, model routing, schema-checked tools, budgets, checkpoints and validators. Code owns exact calculations, state transitions and durable waits. Tools create records or artifacts through authorized interfaces; raw source text never grants authority.

  title:
    Learning
  description:
    Separate case correction, scoped identity/alias, approved conversion or operating rule, and product/model changes. Do not silently generalize exceptions. Shared improvements require appropriate permission and minimization; no cross-tenant memory leakage.

  title:
    Security and recovery
  description:
    Run tenant/matter isolation, revocation and source-filter tests before real data. Give untrusted parsers only scoped assets and restricted capabilities. Define credential recovery, retention/deletion of derivatives and caches, backup handling, and incident/restore ownership.

  title:

  description:

RECOMMENDATIONS CHALLENGED
A. No automatic episode merge merely because document numbers match. A PO may have several deliveries; invoice identifiers recur by supplier/year/business; a matter has multiple hearings. Scoped keys retrieve candidates. Relationship type, cardinality, temporal compatibility and conflicts govern linking. Merge/split lineage must revalidate affected tasks and approvals.
B. No exact-key/reply-only access to closed episodes. A correction such as 'last week's issue was never resolved' needs bounded search by text, time and entities, potentially semantic retrieval. SQL candidate discovery does not replace interpretation.
C. No provenance restricted to code outputs. Persisted interpretations used for tasks, approvals, answers, summaries or artifacts also need evidence and version dependencies. Track operationally consequential derivations, not every prose sentence or hidden reasoning step.
D. No 'ready to pay' label from receipt matching. Payment eligibility requires separately defined authority, terms and checks. Keep reported payment, verified payment and unassessed eligibility distinct.
E. No owner queue as automatic overflow destination. Retry within budget, expose aging, provide operational recovery, then ask a person only when their knowledge or authority is needed.
F. No broad exact-key-only fallback presented as successful understanding. It may support a clearly restricted mode, but missed recall and unsupported cases remain measured and visible.
G. No claim that retrieval cost depends only on open episodes. Index size, history distribution, media, query selectivity and correction fan-out also matter.
H. No universal stop-all-development rule for every fresh failure. Suspend the affected reliance/release scope, preserve failures, diagnose and repair. Required critical checks still gate use.
