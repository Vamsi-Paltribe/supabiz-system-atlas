A correction in emphasis—not a restart

Why these changes were introduced.

Implementation-team feedback exposed an imbalance: controls were specified more precisely than conversation understanding. A second review against the original user vision exposed missing product demonstrations: normal work, direct commands, artifacts, acknowledgement routes and lifecycle recovery. The revisions connect these mechanisms to explicit sprint exits.

Implementation feedback

Meaning needed a mechanism

The build team challenged how the assistant links fragments, interprets files, carries corrections and constructs supported answers. These now have named mechanisms and tests.

Original product intent

Daily assistance needed proof

The cross-check restored explicit demonstrations for normal work, owner voice/text commands, generated documents, authentication recovery and phone–desktop continuity.

Review and debate

Simplification needed limits

Claude identified gaps; we challenged unsafe shortcuts such as automatic merges and “ready to pay” from receipt matching. Accepted changes are recorded below.

Which version governs?

1.3 supersedes the delivery sequence and identified design choices in earlier editions. Unchanged 1.1 controls remain applicable. 1.2 implementation findings remain historical reported evidence, not newly verified code.

This is a design and delivery edition, not newly built product functionality. The other MacBook’s current source and runtime still need inspection.

A decision, its cause, its mechanism and its test

What changed—and why.

12 design decisions shown

D01Understanding becomes an explicit mechanismClarify · S1–S2+
Why it changed
The controls were more concrete than the way fragmented conversations become business meaning.
Chosen mechanism
Contextual retrieval, typed observations, competing interpretations, scoped identity resolution and versioned episode membership. Episodes group evidence and records; they do not own truth.
Proof required
Two crates short → remaining came → photo → other outlet → revised invoice. Retract the wrong relationship, reopen the shortage and retain history.
D02Normal work gets a quiet pathAdd · S1+
Why it changed
An exception-only assistant would miss most daily work and turn the app into an alarm panel.
Chosen mechanism
Process ordinary receipts, counts and bills alongside exceptions. Quiet extraction is not silent acceptance: admission still follows the agreed business policy.
Proof required
A normal complete delivery is correctly reflected without a false discrepancy or unnecessary clarification.
D03Owner commands and voice notes arrive earlyAdd · S2+
Why it changed
The original product promise includes speaking or typing useful work, not only asking questions.
Chosen mechanism
Typed intent with scope, authority, progress and outcome; voice-note transcription with critical-number and negation checks. Consequential effects use the applicable preview/review.
Proof required
Prepare a pending report, assign a follow-up and correct a quantity through the app. Duplicate submissions create one logical request.
D04Documents become versioned outputsAdd · S2+
Why it changed
A PDF can remain in circulation after its source quantity or status changes.
Chosen mechanism
Record template version, input record versions, file hash, audience and supersession. Keep historical files and label their freshness; sharing is a separate authorized action.
Proof required
Correct a quantity after generating the PDF. Mark the tracked report stale/superseded and generate a new version. Do not pretend downloaded copies can be recalled.
D05Choose the acknowledgement channelAdd · S1 trial · S3 complete loop+
Why it changed
We promised assigned work and acknowledgement without choosing how staff receive it while outbound WhatsApp stays deferred.
Chosen mechanism
Trial an enrolled manager on the responsive GoFathom app early. Optional enrolled staff see scoped tasks. Manual relay is labelled and counted; external sends need the appropriate gateway.
Proof required
A manager receives and accepts a specific task version. A message view or manual relay is not automatically acknowledgement.
D06Authentication includes recovery and departureClarify · S0 boundaries · S3 recovery+
Why it changed
Permission checks alone do not cover expired connections, shared phones, staff leaving or orphaned assignments.
Chosen mechanism
Enrollment, scoped grants, connector recovery ownership, device revocation and authorized task reassignment. Recheck current access on pending effects.
Proof required
Revoke a manager while a review is open; reconnect a source without broadening access; leave an assignment pending authorized reassignment.
D07Phone and desktop share one durable stateClarify · S2–S3+
Why it changed
The assistant must continue across devices and survive intermittent connectivity.
Chosen mechanism
Responsive first surface, durable request IDs, version checks and queued/synced/conflicted states. Full native installers and ambient capture remain later.
Proof required
Start on phone, continue on desktop, reconnect an offline device after access is revoked. No duplicate or stale privileged action.
D08Memory must change when evidence changesClarify · S2+
Why it changed
Remembering more is not useful if corrections leave stale tasks, answers or PDFs behind.
Chosen mechanism
Track consequential derivations, including model interpretations, plus scoped queries and watermarks for absence claims. Invalidate affected results; revalidate tasks and approvals.
Proof required
A late credit note invalidates a prior no-credit-note result. A correction reaches a closed episode even without an exact reply or document number.
D09Budget exhaustion is a visible product stateAdd · S1 visibility · S4 load+
Why it changed
Affordability was a formula without a clear user experience when capacity runs out.
Chosen mechanism
Per-tenant budgets, bounded retrieval/retries, queue age and operational recovery. Do not silently drop work or route every overflow to the owner.
Proof required
Exhaust the configured processing budget. Pending work and its consequences stay visible; recovery is deduplicated and policy-qualified.
D10Put safeguards and demand evidence before expansionClarify · S0 onward+
Why it changed
Late security tests or late buyer feedback can invalidate an otherwise polished build.
Chosen mechanism
Isolation tests before real data, plain source/group transparency, a named buyer/reviewer and price hypothesis. Early acknowledgement trial and protected fresh cases.
Proof required
Deny cross-business retrieval and revoked access before intake. Record actual reviewer effort and demand evidence rather than assumed willingness to pay.
D11Keep Ramya in the foundation testsClarify · S0 fixtures · later legal pack+
Why it changed
A restaurant-only implementation could embed assumptions that make matter confidentiality and notice revisions expensive later.
Chosen mechanism
Small synthetic or consented legal identity, permission and revision fixtures. Full Gmail/matter operations follow separate domain qualification.
Proof required
Two matters for one client remain separated; a revised notice changes the right matter only. No inferred statutory deadline.
D12Keep the core; earn each expansionRetain · All sprints+
Why it changed
A new framework or more services does not repair wrong identities, dropped instructions or stale conclusions.
Chosen mechanism
Retain useful TS/Postgres code after inspection. Use modules, isolated parsers and a bounded runner. Preserve the external authority for stock/accounting fields.
Proof required
Replay the same real app path and source-pinned defects. Replace a component only when a measured gap and comparison justify it.

What we rejected in the debate

  • Automatic merge from a matching document number: one PO can cover several deliveries.
  • Searching closed situations only by exact keys: natural-language corrections would be missed.
  • Dependencies only for code outputs: model interpretations can also influence consequential work.
  • “Ready to pay” from receipt matching: payment eligibility is a separate assessment.
  • Sending every retrieval overflow to the owner: that conceals product failure as review work.
The operating loop

Evidence becomes understanding. Understanding becomes work.

The six stations below refine the original factory’s responsibilities. They are modules and states—not six mandatory microservices. Corrections can revisit earlier stations after an episode is closed.

P01

Connect & capture

Know what was observed and what may be missing.

  • Selected sources and current grants
  • Source IDs, revisions, reply chains and original media
  • Connection health, source coverage and processing lag

Output: Durable source observations, not yet business facts

P02

Read & understand

Recover meaning from fragmented text and media.

  • Text/table extraction, image regions and timestamped transcripts
  • Exact-key context first, then bounded text/entity/time/semantic retrieval
  • Observations, intent, references and alternative interpretations

Output: Evidence-linked candidate interpretations

P03

Relate & validate

Maintain a changing account of the situation.

  • Stable episode IDs and proposed/accepted/retracted membership edges
  • Scope, units, times, parent-child admission and authority checks
  • Split/merge lineage and dependency invalidation

Output: Versioned records plus explicit uncertainty

P04

Answer & prepare

Turn reliable state into useful assistance.

  • Code calculations and version-bound critical answer fields
  • Typed/voice commands with scope and progress
  • Versioned PDF/report outputs with private audience

Output: Supported answer or authorized proposed effect

P05

Assign & follow

Make responsibility and progress durable.

  • Chosen human acknowledgement route
  • Owner, due time, blocker and restart-safe timers
  • Current grants and effect checks at execution

Output: Acknowledged work with visible next steps

P06

Verify & improve

Close only when the outcome is supported.

  • Completion evidence and authorized closure
  • Corrections can reopen work and supersede outputs
  • Scoped corrections, approved rules and protected product evaluation

Output: Verified outcome and measured effort

Technical decisions in plain language

What lives inside the loop.

Episode identity+

Stable ID plus versioned membership edges to evidence and typed records. Tasks reference specific records and task versions. Matching document keys retrieve candidates; they do not authorize merge. Split/merge keeps lineage and revalidates affected approvals.

Retrieval and routing+

Use reply chains, scoped identities and document identifiers first. Expand through bounded lexical, entity, time and semantic search where useful, including closed situations. Log search-or-skip reasons; audit skipped events independently. SQL candidates do not replace language interpretation.

Changing evidence+

Positive dependencies cover accepted records, calculations and consequential model-derived interpretations. Negative results retain selector scope and observation watermark. New matching evidence can invalidate a conclusion without correcting any earlier record.

Temporal meaning+

Keep event time, source/document time, effective time and decision time distinct. Unknown dates remain unknown. A current instruction must not be silently dropped because no historical effective date was supplied; activation follows scoped instruction policy.

Authority and concurrency+

Evidence, interpretation, accepted state and authority remain distinct. Current grants and relevant source/effect/policy versions are checked at admission or execution. Re-review is necessary when those change; otherwise avoid redundant approval.

Owner experience+

Needs attention, Work in progress, Ask GoFathom and What GoFathom knows remain proposed surfaces. Cards show evidence, uncertainty, recommended action and exact effect. Investigate first, route to the best-placed authorized person, batch nonurgent questions and expose aging.

Tool and harness boundary+

The harness supplies context, model routing, schema-checked tools, budgets, checkpoints and validators. Code owns exact calculations, state transitions and durable waits. Tools create records or artifacts through authorized interfaces; raw source text never grants authority.

Learning+

Separate case correction, scoped identity/alias, approved conversion or operating rule, and product/model changes. Do not silently generalize exceptions. Shared improvements require appropriate permission and minimization; no cross-tenant memory leakage.

Security and recovery+

Run tenant/matter isolation, revocation and source-filter tests before real data. Give untrusted parsers only scoped assets and restricted capabilities. Define credential recovery, retention/deletion of derivatives and caches, backup handling, and incident/restore ownership.

Modalities and deployment+

Phone/desktop responsive access and voice notes belong in the first useful experience. Cloud workers preserve durable state. Vision supports evidence with locators. Live voice, native bridges, browser workers and video receive workflow-specific entry gates; none is assumed reliable from a provider feature list.

Two businesses, one reusable foundation

What this means for Varun and Ramya.

First bounded workflow

Varun: normal receipt → shortage → correction → closure

  1. A normal delivery is captured and matched without an unnecessary alert.
  2. A different delivery is reported two crates short. The assistant links evidence and preserves unresolved outlet ambiguity.
  3. “Remaining came” and a photo propose a resolution. “No, other outlet” retracts that link and reopens the original shortage.
  4. A revised invoice updates billing evidence; it does not prove goods arrived or payment is eligible.
  5. The enrolled manager acknowledges the specific follow-up. Manual supplier contact is labelled as manual.
  6. A valid later receipt or credit note supports reviewed closure. The pending report is regenerated; earlier artifacts keep their history.

First slice: one outlet, receipt/bill evidence, matches, discrepancies, follow-up and reports. No automatic payments, purchasing, supplier sends or external ledger writeback.

Later domain qualification

Ramya: court notice → correct matter → acknowledged work

  1. An authorized Gmail notice is matched to a matter using scoped identifiers and document evidence.
  2. A second matter for the same client remains separate. Conflicting or incomplete identifiers create a focused question.
  3. An amended notice revises the relevant date/document and marks affected assignments for revalidation.
  4. An authorized staff member receives the correct documents and acknowledges the task version.
  5. Explicit notice dates remain distinct from inferred legal deadlines. Confidentiality follows the matter.
  6. Completion requires appropriate evidence and review; forwarding a document is not proof the work is complete.

Later domain release with its own Gmail, privacy and legal-workflow tests. Early synthetic/consented fixtures check shared identity, revision and grant assumptions only.

S0
Before any real pilot data

Establish the real baseline

Trace one fresh intake through the real UI, worker and saved records; show the selected-source and permission boundary.

Owner: Engineering lead + business reviewer + product owner

  • Pin current code, entrypoints and migrations; distinguish diagnostic paths from the actual app.
  • Create a source-pinned register of known defects and reproduction outcomes.
  • Run cross-business/matter denial, revocation, non-enrolled-source filtering and relevant parser isolation checks before real data; otherwise use synthetic fixtures.
  • Name the buyer, reviewer, existing record authority, communication route and connector recovery owner. Record a price hypothesis, buyer relationship and graded demand evidence.
  • Prepare protected fresh cases, raw-context/summary/manual comparison plans and small legal foundation fixtures.
Exit evidence
  • Enabled capabilities have source evidence or an explicit gap.
  • Real-data access and isolation gates pass; no completeness claim exceeds source coverage.
  • Known failures, baseline effort and first-workflow scope are recorded.

When to pause or narrow: Block unsafe real-data intake or reliance; fix divergent app paths before treating diagnostic results as product proof.

S1
S0 boundaries and baseline

Understand routine and ambiguous intake

One normal delivery and one ambiguous mixed-media delivery produce evidence-linked records or explicit uncertainty.

Owner: Application engineer + domain reviewer

  • Repair applicable numeric normalization, time/instruction handling, parent-child admission, identity/unit links, repeated approval and calculation context defects.
  • Introduce lightweight episode membership and candidate alternatives using existing records.
  • Evaluate required voice/image capability early; show processing budgets, queued work and source/processing freshness.
  • Run a low-fidelity manager acknowledgement trial now, before building the full work loop. Count manual relay effort.
  • Test fresh variations alongside historical regressions; keep every reproduced defect fixed or explicitly deferred with impact and reason.
Exit evidence
  • Routine processing does not create a false exception or unauthorized silent acceptance.
  • Ambiguous links are preserved and clarification is focused.
  • No blocking defect for an enabled capability is hidden by deferral.
  • The acknowledgement route shows credible adoption; budget exhaustion is visible.

When to pause or narrow: Restrict the affected capability if interpretation or clarification burden exceeds the agreed budget. Revise the route if it simply adds a second inbox.

S2
S1 source-grounded interpretation

Handle change and deliver useful outputs

Correct an outlet or quantity days later, ask for current status by voice/text and generate a corrected pending report.

Owner: Application engineer + independent QA

  • Track consequential derivations, scoped absence queries and watermarks. Revisit open and closed situations.
  • Revalidate affected record admission, tasks, approvals and calculations after corrections or new evidence.
  • Validate and render critical answer quantities/statuses from saved versions; evaluate explanatory prose too.
  • Execute a scoped typed/voice-note command through the same product path.
  • Generate one versioned PDF/report with input versions, template, audience, hash and supersession.
Exit evidence
  • The fragment/correction case produces the right current state without rewriting history.
  • A newly arriving credit note invalidates the relevant absence claim.
  • Stale artifacts are labelled; a new report references the corrected records.
  • Commands have visible scope/progress/outcome and duplicate requests are handled.

When to pause or narrow: Suspend affected outputs when a correction leaves unsupported statements, stale approvals or concealed dependencies.

S3
Validated acknowledgement trial and S2 state semantics

Complete work across people and devices

Manager accepts a task, records a blocker, survives a restart, verifies closure and reopens it on contradictory evidence.

Owner: Workflow engineer + manager + QA

  • Implement the chosen actual acknowledgement route and version-specific task acceptance.
  • Persist assignment, due times, reminders, blockers, completion report and closure conditions.
  • Show manual relays honestly; external communications require the relevant effect gateway.
  • Exercise phone-to-desktop continuity, offline queued requests, current authorization on reconnect and version conflicts.
  • Handle departing staff, revoked devices, connector re-linking and authorized reassignment.
Exit evidence
  • Restart and duplicate delivery do not lose or duplicate work.
  • Viewing a message is never automatically task acceptance or completion.
  • Revoked access and stale device submissions cannot create unauthorized effects.
  • The manager can finish the workflow with measured effort.

When to pause or narrow: Pause affected reliance on wrong-audience access, lost work, unsupported closure or adoption failure; repair before expansion.

S4
Complete bounded workflow and applicable prior gates

Prove value, reliability and economics

Run protected fresh episodes and a bounded consented one-outlet shadow beside the existing process.

Owner: Engineering lead + independent evaluator + buyer

  • Compare governed path, strong raw-context and summary baselines and manual work using the same eligible cases.
  • Measure stage-level interpretation, links, omissions, answers, closure, false alarms and total review/clarification effort.
  • Benchmark history growth, affected-graph fan-out, tenant concurrency, tail latency, queue age, storage and total processing cost.
  • Rehearse restore/rollback, source recovery and incidents; broaden security/fault tests without postponing earlier safeguards.
  • Record residual risks, scoped reliance authorizer, reviewer capacity, demand evidence and economics before expansion.
Exit evidence
  • Risk-specific evidence and an explicit reliance decision support the bounded use.
  • Assisted work reduces total burden at acceptable correctness; abstaining on all clear cases cannot pass.
  • Support, processing and reviewer costs fit a credible offer.
  • Remaining limitations and unsupported slices remain visible.

When to pause or narrow: Suspend affected reliance on critical failures or unsustainable effort; preserve results and diagnose. This does not mean stopping every unrelated development task.

CORE DIRECTION RETAINED
Keep verified existing TypeScript/PostgreSQL components. Build context-sensitive interpretation, changing business state and persistent follow-through through the actual application. First outlet workflow covers receiving/bill evidence, ordinary matches, discrepancies, assignment, follow-up, closure and a pending report. Existing stock/accounting authority remains external unless explicitly changed. Do not expand this into a complete payable-approval system.

ADDITIONS ACCEPTED
1. Decide and test the acknowledgement route before claiming end-to-end work. Default pilot candidate: enrolled manager on responsive GoFathom; scoped staff task view where enrolled. Manual manager relay is labelled and its effort counted. External sends and notifications require the appropriate effect boundary. Opening a message is not accepting a task.
2. Quiet normal-work processing alongside exceptions. Extraction and matching do not silently admit business facts outside policy. Preserve discoverability and appropriate sampled quality review.
3. Direct typed and voice-note commands in the first useful vertical slice. Interpret intent, scope, authority and effect; expose progress and cancel/failure state. Avoid redundant confirmation for expressly preauthorized low-risk effects, while consequential changes need the applicable review.
4. One generated report/PDF with template/input versions, provenance, private audience, hash and freshness/supersession. A downloaded file cannot be recalled; mark tracked artifact status and notify authorized recipients where configured, without pretending to revoke outside copies.
5. Account/connector recovery, staff departure, changed phone numbers/shared devices, revoked sessions and orphaned work. Reassignment is authorized, not automatic disclosure to a replacement.
6. Cross-device and reconnect behavior: durable request IDs, stale-version detection, queued/pending/synced/conflict states and current authorization at execution.
7. Visible tenant budgets, bounded retries and retrieval, queue aging and operator recovery. No silent dropping or automatic model fallback that violates data/capability policy.
8. Plain group/source transparency: explain what is read, who can see it, source limitations and recovery. Exact jurisdiction-specific legal obligations require a separate sourced review.
9. Small synthetic or consented legal architecture cases from the start: matter identity, separation and notice revision. These prevent restaurant-specific foundation assumptions; they are not a full legal-pack pilot gate.
10. Observe whether the product moves work to another inbox or causes relevant reporting to leave the selected sources. An absence of source messages does not establish absence of business events.

RECOMMENDATIONS CHALLENGED
A. No automatic episode merge merely because document numbers match. A PO may have several deliveries; invoice identifiers recur by supplier/year/business; a matter has multiple hearings. Scoped keys retrieve candidates. Relationship type, cardinality, temporal compatibility and conflicts govern linking. Merge/split lineage must revalidate affected tasks and approvals.
B. No exact-key/reply-only access to closed episodes. A correction such as 'last week's issue was never resolved' needs bounded search by text, time and entities, potentially semantic retrieval. SQL candidate discovery does not replace interpretation.
C. No provenance restricted to code outputs. Persisted interpretations used for tasks, approvals, answers, summaries or artifacts also need evidence and version dependencies. Track operationally consequential derivations, not every prose sentence or hidden reasoning step.
D. No 'ready to pay' label from receipt matching. Payment eligibility requires separately defined authority, terms and checks. Keep reported payment, verified payment and unassessed eligibility distinct.
E. No owner queue as automatic overflow destination. Retry within budget, expose aging, provide operational recovery, then ask a person only when their knowledge or authority is needed.
F. No broad exact-key-only fallback presented as successful understanding. It may support a clearly restricted mode, but missed recall and unsupported cases remain measured and visible.
G. No claim that retrieval cost depends only on open episodes. Index size, history distribution, media, query selectivity and correction fan-out also matter.
H. No universal stop-all-development rule for every fresh failure. Suspend the affected reliance/release scope, preserve failures, diagnose and repair. Required critical checks still gate use.

FINAL CLAUDE RESPONSE AND ADJUDICATION
Claude accepted the seven challenged qualifications and withdrew the exact-key automatic-merge, closed-episode retrieval restriction, code-output-only dependency, receipt-match payment-readiness and unverified legal-duty prescriptions. It retained five sequencing objections and a budget-visibility timing point. Adopt these as follows:
1. S0 creates a source-pinned failure register. S1 must show each reproduced defect fixed through the app or explicitly deferred with impact and reason; no required capability can pass its gate while its blocking defect is deferred. The feedback calls these eight classes but groups several together, so the register must enumerate actual distinct defects rather than repeat an unverified count. Named reported defects include rule effective-time handling, numeric normalization, parent-child admission consistency, stale/repeated approval, missing deterministic comparisons, outlet/item/unit linking and gross/net/payable/payment semantics.
2. Run a low-fidelity manager acknowledgement trial in S1 or S2 before implementing the complete S3 work loop. Count manual relay and whether users feel they acquired a second inbox. The full S3 route is conditional on this evidence.
3. S0 records buyer relationship, stated value, named reviewer capacity and a priced pilot proposition or price hypothesis. Do not invent willingness to pay. Record graded demand evidence and revise at sprint exits; payment is not a prerequisite for bounded discovery.
4. Run cross-business/matter-scope denial, revocation, non-enrolled-content filtering and relevant parser isolation tests BEFORE real data enters. S4 broadens verification; it is not the first security gate. If S0 is not ready, use synthetic data.
5. Log decisions to search or skip historical/closed episodes, with concise operational reason codes, retrieval scope and outcomes. Audit skipped routes using independently labelled examples so the evaluation measures missed searches as well as bad results. Reason logs are not proof that a skip was correct.
6. Make budget exhaustion, pending work and queue age visible in S1. S4 load testing checks sustained behavior.

PERFORMANCE AND COST CHECK
Symbols: N indexed records, K retrieved candidates, B newly observed asset bytes, V_a affected derived records, E_a affected dependency edges.
Intake work includes O(B) reading of new bytes plus index/storage operations. Normal balanced-tree lookup is approximately O(log N + K) under suitable indexes; broad filters may scan O(N). These are data-structure bounds, not measured application latency.
Dependency traversal with visited-node deduplication is O(V_a + E_a) for the affected graph; worst-case changes can affect the full graph. Cycles need controlled handling and idempotent processing. Selective invalidation is a goal to measure, not an O(1) guarantee.
Store source revisions, assets and dependency relationships with declared retention; storage is driven by retained bytes and relationships, not just message count. Deletion must address derivatives, indexes, caches and backups under a defined policy.
Measure tokens and model time separately from database complexity. Track intake plus extraction plus retrieval plus answering plus retries, not just query cost. Benchmark realistic history lengths and tenant concurrency, including tail latency, queue lag, correction recall, memory/storage and reviewer minutes. Do not invent a performance improvement percentage.
Vendor cash cost, customer human effort and combined cost remain separate; the subscription is not counted twice as combined resource cost. Add support/reconnect effort and failed/abandoned work to the business-value assessment.
