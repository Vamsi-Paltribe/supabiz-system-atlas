Why
An ambiguous follow-up must not silently resolve a delivery or change accepted quantities.
Decision

CL02Make absence claims aware of processing and backfill+
Why
A document dated before an old watermark can arrive or become readable later. Source timestamps cannot establish processing completeness.
Decision
Bind a persisted absence result to its query scope, authorized source scope, observed revisions, processing/extraction state and relevant interpretation version. Re-evaluate affected results on backfill, attachment completion, extractor change or source/grant change.
Qualification
Do not promise complete business awareness. Disclose pending/failed extraction and unknown coverage. Broad processing changes may invalidate a broad set; uncertain matches widen the candidate review scope instead of silently excluding evidence.

Why
A credit note can settle billing without restoring stock; a replacement can restore stock without settling billing.
Decision
Use scoped resolution records and explicit task predicates on the existing discrepancy/task model. Record which obligation is resolved and which remains open.
Qualification
A write-off is an authorized acceptance of loss, not a receipt. An operational decision is not an accounting posting in an external system. Partial evidence resolves only the supported quantity or amount.

CL03 / task-specific resolution

What does “closed” actually mean?

A task closes against its own evidence and authority conditions. The physical and billing obligations can finish at different times. Extend the existing discrepancy/task model only where the audit identifies a gap.

O01Replacement received+
Required evidence
Admitted receiving evidence for the correct delivery, outlet, item, quantity and unit; identify the original shortage it addresses.
Required authority
Receiving admission policy or authorized reviewer.
Physical meaning
Reduce the supported outstanding physical quantity; partial replacements leave a remainder.
Billing meaning
No automatic settlement of a price, invoice or credit dispute.
Closure predicate
Close only the physical-replacement obligation when its required quantity is supported.
O02Credit accepted+
Required evidence
Matching credit evidence, scope and amount, validated and accepted under the business policy.
Required authority
Authorized billing reviewer or approved admission policy.
Physical meaning
No stock increase or claim that goods arrived.
Billing meaning
Resolve only the covered billing difference; retain any uncovered amount or other billing issue.
Closure predicate
Close the credit/billing task when its acceptance conditions hold; physical replenishment may remain open.
O03Loss accepted / written off+
Required evidence
Explicit decision recording the unresolved quantity/value, reason, scope and referenced evidence.
Required authority
Person or policy specifically authorized to accept that loss; not merely the original reporter.
Physical meaning
Goods remain missing; record accepted loss separately from receipt.
Billing meaning
Accounting treatment remains subject to the authoritative system and its posting approval.
Closure predicate
Close the loss-disposition task if authorized; do not label replacement received or credit issued.
O04Finding corrected+
Required evidence
Admitted correction invalidating the original discrepancy, with corrected source relationships and calculations.
Required authority
Correction admission policy or authorized reviewer.
Physical meaning
Recompute the supported physical result; do not invent a compensating receipt.
Billing meaning
Recompute only affected billing checks.
Closure predicate
Close the invalidated finding as corrected; revalidate dependent tasks and preserve the previous history.
O05Still unresolved / partially resolved+
Required evidence
Missing evidence, unresolved identity, partial replacement/credit or unconfirmed authority.
Required authority
No closure authority is inferred from uncertainty.
Physical meaning
Outstanding physical obligations remain visible.
Billing meaning
Outstanding financial obligations remain visible.
Closure predicate
Keep the affected tasks open or explicitly blocked; do not collapse partial resolution into a blanket closed flag.
Correct outcome

Credit accepted; stock still short

“The supplier credit has resolved the billing difference. The physical shortage remains; replenishment is still pending.”

Incorrect conclusion

Everything is closed

A credit note is not receiving evidence. An accepted loss is not replacement stock. A reported decision is not an external accounting posting.

CL05 / one case set and runner

Measure meaning, workflow and real effort separately.

Scorecard 1

Understanding

  • Identity and business scope
  • Quantities, units and field meaning
  • Relationship accuracy and ambiguity
  • Correction propagation and late evidence
  • Supporting evidence and calibrated uncertainty
Scorecard 2

Workflow

  • False alerts
  • Clarification requests and repeated questions
  • Owner/reviewer interventions
  • Incorrect or premature closure
  • Assignment acknowledgement and durable follow-through
Format validity and write validity are separate checks

Record output-format validity and product-write validation separately from semantic quality. Invalid writes cannot pass as successful effects. If meaning is not assessable, record that explicitly rather than awarding semantic credit.

Compare raw retrieval, summary plus exact lookup and the governed approach with the same model/version, evidence-availability cutoff and comparable generic tools, permissions and task instructions. Disclose any remaining tool or budget differences. Include preprocessing, extraction, indexing, summarization, retrieval, answering, retries and human review.

Keep one development scenario separate from unseen variants and episode families. Near-duplicates should test real identity/time/relationship confusion. Do not repeatedly tune against protected results and continue calling them untouched holdouts.

Synthetic counts are proxies. Actual time savings, adoption and the second-inbox effect require observed user trials with named participants. Do not invent percentage improvements.

Use evidence availability—not merely document date—as the cutoff. A backdated credit note must not appear in an evaluation arm before its simulated import or extraction event.

AT07Replacement, partial resolution and lossUnderstanding + workflow+
Setup
Partial replacement arrives, then an authorized person accepts the remaining loss. A separate billing question remains.
Expected behavior
Reduce only the supported physical quantity; classify remainder as accepted loss. Do not claim all goods arrived or billing settled. A non-authorized reporter cannot approve the loss.

Setup

Setup

Record semantic credit separately. Reject the invalid write and log format failure; do not erase semantic evidence or block the unrelated question.
